## Research note

**Roc Sastre** · MSc Financial Economics, Barcelona School of Economics

This note examines what successful computation can establish in a small US
Bayesian vector autoregression, a model used to study relationships among
macroeconomic variables. Computational checks and monitored convergence passed,
but residual evidence left important conditions for substantive interpretation
unmet. The case study shows why computational success and model adequacy need
to be assessed separately.

The contribution is specific to this application. Substantive impulse responses
remain withheld; the note makes no causal policy claim and does not establish a
validated alternative model.

## Materials and verification

The diagnostic replication package preserves the approved research-note PDF,
source, diagnostic records and a small verification script. It checks the
integrity and consistency of preserved records; it does not rerun estimation
or empirical diagnostics. Raw data, posterior objects and substantive
impulse-response estimates are excluded.
